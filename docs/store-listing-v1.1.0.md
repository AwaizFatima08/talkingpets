## English (en-US)

**App name** (30 max)
TalkyPals: Talking Pets

**Short description** (80 max)
Talking 3D pets for toddlers! They repeat your child's words in funny voices.

**Full description**
Meet five cuddly 3D friends who love to listen and talk back!

Say something and Mimi the kitten, Kiwi the parrot, Doodle the rooster, Biscuit the puppy or Peanut the elephant repeats it in a silly, squeaky voice. Little ones giggle as their words come back from their favourite pet.

TAP, TICKLE AND PLAY
• Tap a head, tummy, tail, ear, nose, beak or trunk. Every spot has its own sound and reaction.
• Pets smile, jump, wiggle, blush, sneeze and fill the screen with hearts, stars and bubbles.
• Biscuit woofs and pants, and Peanut trumpets and stomps.

FIVE 3D WORLDS
• A cosy playroom for Mimi
• A leafy jungle for Kiwi
• A sunny farm for Doodle
• A backyard with a doghouse for Biscuit
• A golden savanna for Peanut

MADE FOR TODDLERS
• Big, simple buttons and no reading needed
• No login, no account and no chat
• The microphone is used only while the app is open. Voices are played back straight away and are never saved or sent.
• The app is free and shows ads suitable for children (rated G). A settings area for grown-ups sits behind a parent gate.

Great for speech play, first words and lots of laughs.

Some sounds are Powered by Stability AI.
